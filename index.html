Who You'll Work With

Rooted in
who you are.

Choosing the right therapist requires more than professional qualifications alone. An effective therapeutic relationship combines rigorous clinical training with a genuine appreciation of the values, culture, and lived experiences that shape a person's life. As a Licensed Clinical Social Worker and ordained Rabbi, I offer evidence-based therapy informed by both clinical expertise and an insider's understanding of the challenges that people face. Combining extensive experience in mental health and addiction recovery enables me to provide care that is compassionate, culturally informed, and clinically effective.

What you gain is not only theory. It is a toolkit refined through hundreds of real-life cases: relationship conflict, family breakdown, crisis, grief, identity, addiction, and resilience. Whatever you are carrying, you will be genuinely seen, spoken to with honesty, and given something real to work with. You should leave with more clarity, more capacity, and a clearer sense of what is possible for you.

You can meet in person in Johannesburg or online from wherever you are; clients currently connect from the UK, USA, Israel, Australia, and beyond.

How It Works

How the work
actually happens.

The more you put in, the more you get out — though it does ask for real time, honest effort, and emotional energy.

01

Bring the work and receive the tools.

You will get practical frameworks, evidence-based techniques, and honest feedback which you can take into your life and use immediately, not just talk about.

02

The whole person, not just the problem.

Your situation does not exist in isolation. The work takes in the psychological, the relational, and the deeper dimensions of meaning and purpose, so nothing important gets left outside the room.

03

Honest and direct.

You need to feel seen, heard, and genuinely understood before you can be meaningfully challenged. That foundation comes first. Then the work gets direct: honest feedback, a push when it matters, and steady movement forward.
