Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what happens with your information when you visit this website or get in touch through it.

What this site collects

This website itself does not run any tracking software and does not use Google Analytics or similar third-party analytics tools. No cookies are set for advertising or tracking purposes.

The contact form

The "Book a Session" form asks for your first and last name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in (optional), and any message you'd like to add (optional).

This form does not submit to a server or database. When you fill it in and press send, it opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled as a message, which you then choose to send yourself — or you can email directly instead. Nothing is stored by this website; your message goes straight to WhatsApp or your email client, the same as if you had messaged or emailed directly.

This form is not for urgent matters or clinical information. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line rather than using this website.

Contacting via WhatsApp or email

Messages sent via WhatsApp or email are subject to the privacy practices of those platforms (Meta/WhatsApp, or your email provider). Any information you choose to share is used only to respond to you and arrange a session — it is not shared with third parties or used for marketing.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Loading a webpage's fonts may involve your browser making a request to Google's servers; no other third-party service is embedded on this site.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally as the site changes. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach out via email or WhatsApp.
